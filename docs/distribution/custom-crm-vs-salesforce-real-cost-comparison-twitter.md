1/ Salesforce Enterprise is $165/user/month. The 3-year cost for a 10-person team, with admin and customization included, runs $160k to $310k. The license is the smallest surprise. ↓

2/ Year 1 for a 10-person Enterprise team: $19,800 in licenses + $20k-$45k implementation + data migration + training = $44,800 to $82,800. Before anyone logs a single contact.

3/ The line most early estimates miss: the Salesforce admin. A fractional admin costs $30k-$60k/year. Over 36 months, that's $60k to $180k added to the comparison before any new feature work.

4/ Salesforce-certified developers bill at $150-$350/hr. CPQ implementation: $30k-$120k. A custom Lightning Web Component: $8k-$40k. These costs don't appear in the license comparison. They appear in Q2 invoices.

5/ Custom CRM build from a boutique studio: $45k-$120k. Retainer: $1.5k-$4k/month. In a worked 3-year model for a 12-person team with two product lines, the custom path totaled $163k. Salesforce: $234k. Difference: $71k.

6/ Salesforce wins at 40+ seats with standard B2B workflows. Custom wins when year-1 Salesforce customization exceeds $30k or the workflow requires separate pipeline models. That's the actual threshold.
