1/ Most AI development proposals arrive before the partner has asked about your data. That's your first signal on fit. ↓

2/ There are 4 engagement models in 2026, and they price very differently:
- Boutique AI studio: $25K-$120K
- Large agency: $120K-$350K
- Freelance AI specialist: $120-$220/hr
- Embedded tech lead: $1,200-$2,400/day

3/ Most AI projects that go wrong chose the wrong engagement model, not the wrong vendor within a model. A freelancer cannot manage a 6-month multi-system integration. A large agency cannot deliver a 2-week prototype at a cost that makes sense.

4/ 4 red flags before you sign:
- Fine-tuning proposed before data is audited
- Scope doc describes architecture, not deliverables
- No production references (or all are "in progress")
- No mention of how the system will fail

5/ A paid discovery phase ($3,500-$8,000) should produce a scope doc, data readiness assessment, and a fixed-price estimate. If it doesn't fit, you exit having spent a fraction of the full project cost.

6/ Ask for the total cost of their last comparable project, including change orders and the first 90 days post-delivery. That gap between estimate and final cost tells you more than any reference call.
