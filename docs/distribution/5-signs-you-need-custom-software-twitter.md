1/ Your team is probably paying for the software twice. Once on the invoice, and once in the hours spent on workarounds, reconciliation, and checking that integrations didn't break overnight. Five signals it's time to build instead. ↓

2/ Signal 1: The shadow system. Your team maintains a spreadsheet alongside the official record because the official record can't represent your data the way the business works. That spreadsheet is the spec for a better tool.

3/ Signal 2: Workflow distortion. Your team changed how it works to fit what the software can record. The original process was better for customers. No one logged the change. These decisions don't appear in any process audit.

4/ Signal 3: Subscription breadth. Teams use 10-20% of the features they pay for. PM tool + client portal + billing tool at small-team rates = $3,642/year before any integration costs.

5/ Signals 4 + 5: Integration labor eats 4-6 hours/week in checks and reruns that never appear on any invoice. Vendor roadmap divergence means the gap between what the software does and what you need becomes structurally permanent.

6/ The diagnostic: total your subscription cost + integration labor (hrs/week × 50 × hourly rate) + process drag. Over $25k/year: payback in 4-5 years. Over $50k: 2-3 years. Builds run $15k-$40k for a focused tool; $60k-$150k to consolidate 2-4 SaaS tools.
